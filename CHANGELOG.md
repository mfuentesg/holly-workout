# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Infinite work/rest interval timer (default 45 s / 15 s, adjustable 5–300 s in 5 s steps)
- iPhone app with pulsing circle and round counter
- Apple Watch app with progress fill and large countdown
- Live Activity on the lock screen, Dynamic Island and watch Smart Stack
- Two-way start / pause / stop sync between iPhone and watch (WatchConnectivity)
- Haptic cues, with queued notifications on the watch when the app is not frontmost
